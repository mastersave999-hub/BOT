## 2. CORE GAME MECHANICS & ZONE ARCHITECTURE
### 2.1 Zones
1. **Avatar Zone**: Up to 4 Avatars, or up to 6 total including Tokens. Cannot overwrite when full unless summon effect clears a slot.
2. **Magic Zone**: Unlimited capacity. Holds Normal/React during resolution, Modification Magic attached to Avatars, and Avatars relocated by effects.
3. **Construct Zone**: Up to 3 Constructs per player, distinct by name.
4. **Land Magic Zone**: 1 shared slot on the entire board. Playing a new Land Magic sends the existing one to Hell.
5. **Deck Zone**: 50 cards exact. Order is private and randomized. Cannot be manually reordered.
6. **Hell Zone (นรก)**: Graveyard. Order must be preserved. Public information.
7. **Dark Dimension Zone (มิติมืด)**: Banishment/exile zone for cards removed via `เนรเทศ`.
8. **LIFE Card Zone**: 5 face-down cards arranged horizontally from top to bottom.

### 2.2 Turn Structure
1. **Draw Phase**:
   - Turn player simultaneously awakens all resting Avatars and draws cards.
   - Hand < 3: Draw until hand size is 3. Hand >= 3: Draw 1 card.
   - P1 Turn 1: Draws 2 cards.
2. **Main Phase**:
   - Unlimited Avatar and Construct summons (subject to GEM/Cost).
   - Magic card usage: strictly **1 per Magic Subtype per turn** (Normal, Modification, React, Land).
   - Trigger unresolved LIFE card effects revealed from the previous turn.
3. **Battle Phase** (Skipped on P1 Turn 1):
   - **Step 1 (Attack Declaration)**: Declare attacking Avatar (tap/rest), optional Unity (สามัคคี) or Backstab (แทงหลัง), declare target (Avatar, Construct, or LIFE Zone if eligible or using เตะไข่).
   - **Step 2 (Snapshot & Attack Triggers)**: Base stats calculate into Layer 3 snapshot; triggers for "เมื่อโจมตี" fire.
   - **Step 3 (Defense & Redirect)**: Defending player may activate Human Shield (โล่มนุษย์).
   - **Step 4 (Combat Resolution)**: Layer 4 React cards and combat modifiers resolve to produce Net POWER.
   - **Step 5 (Damage & Destruction)**: Loser is destroyed and sent to Hell; ties destroy both (unless 0 vs 0, where neither is destroyed).
4. **End Phase**:
   - End-of-turn continuous effects expire.
   - If hand size > 7, discard down to 7 (this is the absolute final action before turn handover).
